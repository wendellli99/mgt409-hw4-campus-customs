# Storefront design

Campus Customs uses Yale navy, warm ivory, and a restrained gold accent, with large serif headlines and clean sans-serif product details. It feels connected to New Haven without imitating the original store’s wording or claiming a live checkout.

The home page presents real catalogue photographs in an offset editorial composition. Every featured image links to its actual item. Collection invitations lead to filtered products, while clear prices, short descriptions, and consistent image frames make comparisons easy.

The product page pairs a generous image with the full description, recorded colors, and selectable size buttons showing exact stock. Unavailable sizes are disabled. A contextual “Ask about this item” action makes uncertainty easy to resolve before a purchase decision.

The floating shop assistant shares the storefront’s visual language. Suggested questions, a visible current-item label, readable answers, product links, loading feedback, and retry controls keep chat useful rather than decorative. Search responses also create product cards in the main page.

Small screens have a dedicated navigation menu, two-column product grids, stacked detail and account pages, and a chat panel sized to the visible viewport. Labels, visible keyboard focus, a skip link, Escape-to-close chat, and reduced-motion support help more shoppers use the experience comfortably.
